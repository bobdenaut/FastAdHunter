Major faults are structurally near-zero — nothing FastAdHunter touches is demand-paged from disk, so a non-zero value means host memory pressure, not a FastAdHunter problem. The committed pair comes from whichever allocator is linked in and carries no compatibility promise, which is why it is labelled and parked here rather than charted beside the kernel readings.

The stack sums to RSS and residual is the top band, so a leak reads as one thing: the top thickening while the others stay flat. The hump here is residual alone — the three components are flat across the whole window. That is the allocator, not the engine. The compile peak never appears in the area because it lasts seconds against a 60 s sample interval: the sampled series has never exceeded 97 MiB while the high-water mark caught 133.7. That is why peak is charted beside the stack, not derived from it. Cache and stats are hairlines at this scale. That is the true proportion, not a rendering fault — read their exact figures in the composition card above. 24H draws every sample. It asks for max_points=1440, so each 60 s reading is a verbatim point. 7D and 30D are past the 5,000-point cap, so the reader keeps every 4th and every 16th sample and reports the stride — the header chip carries it. Decimation never averages a spike away, but it can step over one: anything shorter than the stride was never in this series. Peak RSS is the channel that catches those. RSS is the stack's top edge, in ink — in a stack that sums to RSS, that line is RSS, so it needs no colour of its own. It turns red only above 122.1 MiB, the 128 MB steady-state budget, with 3 MiB of hysteresis so it cannot strobe on the boundary. The shaded band below that starts at 100 MiB — above anything the sampled series has ever recorded, so a reading in it is new territory for this process while still under budget. Three encodings, not five colours. Ruleset is blue, cache and stats are two steps of one aqua, residual is the hatch. Four categorical hues cannot be told apart here — every fourth candidate measured under the colour-blind separation floor against the first three — so the fourth and fifth series are carried by texture and by ink instead.
