Health

Is it serving, and what has gone wrong — GET /health plus the counters that explain it

Serving — status degraded

Under the adaptive upstream strategy, degraded means no endpoint is currently healthy — every one is penalized or being probed. It does not mean down. Cache hits and serve-stale keep answering, and a penalized endpoint is still queried when nothing better remains. Two of the three endpoints are expected back on their next successful probe.

The split matters when something is wrong: synthesized means FastAdHunter could not get an answer and said so; relayed means an upstream answered with an error and FastAdHunter passed it on unchanged. The first points here, the second points at the resolver being used. These are also persisted per interval, so "how many clients saw an error during that outage" survives a restart.

Backpressure and refusals

Events dropped is one number covering both pipelines — they share a single bounded channel, so splitting it would invent a distinction the engine does not make. Non-zero means the event stream shed load, not that queries were dropped. Refused is the egress policy stopping a request before any upstream contact — an unusable Host, or a destination outside the allowed set. Since refusals log at debug, this counter is the only standing signal that a LAN client is probing.

What this page will never do

No message store

There is no diagnostics inbox to accumulate warnings. Everything here is a live counter or a current status, read fresh — nothing to mark as read and nothing to go stale.

No log viewer

No endpoint serves log files, so none is offered. Logs stay where the container puts them.

No red without a reason

degraded is amber and explained, because the box is still answering. A status is only escalated when clients would actually notice.
