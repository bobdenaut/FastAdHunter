## Scope

1. **CONFIGURATION.md** `[dns.upstreams]` section only: `strategy` gains
   `adaptive` (opt-in, default unchanged), `penalty_failures` with default,
   env names, `timeout_ms`'s validated range `1..=10000` and its per-leg
   meaning (an attempt is bounded at `3 × timeout_ms`). Nothing about
   `PENALTY_BASE`/`PENALTY_MAX` as keys — they are not keys.
2. **API.md** `/telemetry` upstream sample: the seven new fields; `state`
   meaningful under `adaptive` only; `penalized_seconds_total` defined as
   scheduled unavailability — **the nominal `penalty(round)` banked at the
   transition into Penalized, not elapsed wall-clock time, and with the
   ±25 % deadline jitter excluded**: it does not advance while an endpoint
   sits Penalized, so a flat figure through an outage is not an expired
   penalty; actual unavailability can exceed it (no traffic claims the probe
   after the deadline) or fall short of it (a stale in-flight success
   restores Healthy early); probe counted in
   `attempts`/`failures`/`failure_runs`, failed probe in `penalties`;
   `attempts`/`failures` exclude `resolve_host` under `adaptive`;
   `failure_runs` under `adaptive` counts throttled attempts, not time;
   `consecutive_failures` demoted to diagnostic; `/health` degraded = no
   endpoint healthy.
3. **docs/measurement-traps.md** §Traffic and rates, "Upstream `failures` as
   client timeouts" entry: counters grow more slowly during an outage because
   penalized endpoints are skipped; `failure_runs` is attempts, not time.
4. **CONTEXT.md**: endpoint, Healthy / Penalized / Probing, probe, penalty,
   penalty round, policy (Stage 1 sense). No Stage 2/3 terms.
