# P2.6-09 — Injected-Failure Bench (G3)

**Phase:** 2.6 · **Depends on:** p2.6-05, p2.6-02, p2.6-06 · **Model:** Opus

## Goal

Every S1-G3 scenario passes against `fallback` in the same session, and the
behavioural win is reported as net timeout cost avoided in two rows —
client-visible forwards and SWR refreshes — not as a raw attempt count.

## Context

Spec S1-G3 (scenario table and §Net timeout cost avoided), S1.15 (failure
scenarios), S1.6 policy (scaled constants make "recovery within
`PENALTY_MAX`" benchable in milliseconds), S1.9 (SWR claims most probes).
Benchmark plan: docs/design/adaptive-upstream-selection-benchmarks.md.

## Scope

- Harness: mock endpoints that black-hole (timeout), refuse (`ECONNREFUSED`
  via a closed UDP port on Linux, a closed TCP port for a DoT endpoint on
  every platform), answer after a delay, and flap on a schedule; a scaled
  `Policy` injected through the pool constructor.
- Scenarios: black-holed primary + healthy secondary; ICMP-unreachable
  primary; recovery after the endpoint returns with the secondary healthy
  throughout; concurrent probe claims; flapping endpoint; `resolve_host`
  against a due endpoint.
- Measure per scenario, both strategies: queries paying (latency ≥ one leg,
  `timeout_ms`), the measured time each paid, failed probes, client p99, and
  `net_avoided = (fallback_paying − adaptive_paying) × attempt_bound_ms −
  failed_probes × attempt_bound_ms` with `attempt_bound_ms = ATTEMPT_LEGS ×
  timeout_ms` as the cap and the measured figure beside it, reported
  separately for client forwards and for SWR refreshes, with the
  cache-hit-adjusted reading.
- Results file: `docs/code-review/phase2.6/p2.6-09-injected-failure-bench-review.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Black-hole, sequential queries: ≤ `penalty_failures` queries pay up to
  `attempt_bound_ms` (one leg, `timeout_ms`, on these UDP mocks); every
  later query answers at the healthy endpoint's RTT.
- Unreachable: ≤ 1 query pays anything — UDP arm on Linux, DoT arm on every
  platform; the UDP arm on Windows is not a result.
- Recovery: Healthy within `penalty_max` + one query interval with the
  secondary Healthy throughout.
- Never more than one probe in flight per endpoint; never more than one
  probe per query.
- All dead: no query without a packet; a forced attempt landing on a
  Penalized word leaves deadline and round unchanged.
- `resolve_host` against a due endpoint claims nothing.
- Flapping: backoff grows to `penalty_max`, no probe storm, client p99 no
  worse than the steady-outage case.
- Net timeout cost avoided stated in two rows with the formula's inputs.
- Gates green.

## Out of scope

On-device runs (`p2.6-11`); any hedge or RTT measurement.
